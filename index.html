What the data says

  • Normal days are fine. Service level sits around 82%, the forecast lands within about 5%, and very few people hang up.
  • The festive week is where it falls apart. Volume came in about 35% higher but the forecast only planned for 15%. Service level dropped to around 55%, abandons more than doubled and not one day hit target.
  • It wasn't just volume. Contacts got a little longer and more people were off the floor than planned, so the gap got bigger than the forecast miss alone.
  • Mondays are the heaviest, and every queue has two busy spells, late morning and early evening. Those are the hours to keep breaks and training away from.
  • Next time: base the peak uplift on last year's actual peak, freeze leave and training for that window, and line up a temporary batch.
